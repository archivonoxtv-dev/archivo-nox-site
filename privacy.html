Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Overview

Archivo Nox is an independent content project. Its publishing tools are used by the project owner to authorize and publish content to connected social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

When a social platform account is connected, the integration may process basic account information provided by that platform, authorization tokens, and information required to publish content on behalf of the authorized account owner.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, prepare or publish content requested by the account owner, and maintain the publishing workflow.

4. Data Sharing

Archivo Nox does not sell personal data. Information is shared only with the relevant platform when necessary to provide the requested publishing functionality or when required by law.

5. Data Retention and Revocation

Authorization data is retained only as needed to operate the integration. Users may revoke platform access through the connected platform's account or security settings.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and access tokens used by the publishing workflow.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: archivonoxtv@gmail.com
